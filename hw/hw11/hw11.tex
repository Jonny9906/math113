\begin{problem}[26.4]
    Give addition and multiplication tables for $2\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$. Are $2\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}_4$ isomorphic?
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
    The order of the factor group is 4, while the order of $4\mathbb{Z}$ is infnite. Therefore there can not exist a bijection due to difference in order, and in turn $2\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z} \not\cong 4\mathbb{Z}$.
\end{solution}
